Greetings!

We are **[program and year level]** students of **[complete name of school]** currently preparing our capstone project tentatively entitled **“WorkPulse: Employee Attendance and Work Analytics Information System for MVL.”**

We learned that MVL is considering the possible use of fingerprint technology for employee attendance. This information inspired us to explore the development of a proposed web-based system that may help centralize employee information, attendance records, leave requests, payroll information, and related workforce reports. The proposed system may also include fingerprint verification for employee time-in and time-out, subject to MVL's permission, the availability of suitable hardware, appropriate privacy safeguards, and the results of actual development and testing.

At present, **WorkPulse has not yet been fully developed, deployed, or adopted by MVL**. We are still in the initial requirements-gathering and planning stage. We are therefore respectfully requesting permission to conduct an initial study at MVL to understand its existing attendance and workforce-information processes and determine whether the proposed system would be relevant to its actual needs.

Subject to your approval, the initial study may include:

1. a brief interview with the authorized manager or representative regarding the current attendance, employee-record, leave, payroll, and reporting processes;
2. observation of relevant workflows without interfering with normal operations;
3. distribution of a short needs-assessment questionnaire to eligible adult employees who voluntarily agree to participate; and
4. collection of general system requirements, concerns, and preferences that may guide the design of the proposed system.

This initial activity will **not involve fingerprint enrollment, fingerprint scanning, or the collection of biometric data**. Any future biometric activity will require a separate request, a clear privacy notice, informed consent where applicable, an approved testing procedure, and appropriate safeguards for the collection, storage, access, retention, and deletion of biometric information. Participation in interviews or questionnaires will be voluntary, and an employee may decline or withdraw without penalty.

The information gathered will be used solely for academic research and system-planning purposes. Individual responses will be treated confidentially and presented only in summarized or anonymized form. We will avoid collecting unnecessary personal, payroll, authentication, or other sensitive information. The name of MVL and any organizational information will be used in the manuscript only with appropriate authorization.

We also understand that permission to conduct the initial study does not require MVL to purchase, deploy, or adopt WorkPulse. Any future demonstration, evaluation, or possible use of the system will be discussed separately after a working prototype has been developed and reviewed.

We hope for your favorable consideration. We are willing to meet at your preferred time to explain the proposed study, its scope, and the measures that will be followed to protect the organization and its personnel.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
