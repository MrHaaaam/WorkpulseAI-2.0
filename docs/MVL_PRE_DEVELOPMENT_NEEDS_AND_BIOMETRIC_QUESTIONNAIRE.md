# MVL Pre-Development Needs and Attendance-Verification Questionnaire

## Purpose

Good day!

We are initially gathering information for the proposed **WorkPulse Employee Attendance and Work Analytics Information System for MVL**. The system or some of its features may not yet be available. You do not need to have used a prototype to answer this questionnaire.

Your answers will help identify current work processes, common problems, desired system features, and preferred attendance-verification methods. This questionnaire does not collect a fingerprint, face image, or biometric template, and completing it does not mean that you consent to future biometric enrollment.

Participation is voluntary. Answers will be used for research and system-planning purposes. Please do not write passwords, payroll amounts, government identification numbers, biometric information, or other confidential details.

## Suggested Administration

- Target: approximately 10–20 MVL personnel for the initial needs assessment, subject to adviser approval.
- Respondents: eligible MVL manager, regular employees, and student or part-time employees who voluntarily consent.
- Timing: before prototype testing or final system evaluation.
- Analysis: frequency and percentage for closed questions; recurring themes for open-ended answers.

## Researcher Reminders

- Ask the adviser and qualified validators to review the instrument before administration.
- Explain fingerprint, face, both, and non-biometric options neutrally. Do not promise perfect speed, accuracy, or security.
- Do not ask initial respondents to rate the usability, reliability, or overall acceptability of a prototype they have not used.
- Do not treat survey preference as proof that a biometric method is technically effective.
- Do not use “current AI” as a questionnaire choice because it is ambiguous. Name the actual options: descriptive analytics, fixed rule/statistical methods, trained AI, both, or none.
- Do not describe fixed statistical methods as automatically inferior to trained AI. Evaluate suitability according to data availability, transparency, accuracy, privacy, risk, and the intended decision.
- Preference responses do not prove that either a trained model or a fixed method is accurate. Each implemented method requires separate technical evaluation.
- AI-assisted outputs must support human review and must not independently impose disciplinary, payroll, or employment decisions.
- Do not treat survey participation as consent for biometric collection or enrollment.
- Report the actual number invited, number who responded, and number of valid responses.
- For a 10–20-person initial group, use descriptive results and avoid claims that the findings represent people outside MVL.
