# WorkPulse User Evaluation Questionnaire

## Introduction

Good day!

Thank you for participating in our Capstone Project entitled **“WorkPulse: Employee Attendance and Work Analytics Information System for MVL.”**

As one of the intended users of WorkPulse, your feedback after using and exploring the system is important to the review and improvement of the project. Your experience will help the researchers develop a more efficient, reliable, secure, and user-friendly system for employee attendance and work-information management.

This survey is short and confidential. There are no right or wrong answers. Please answer honestly based on your actual experience using WorkPulse. Your responses will be used for research purposes and will help the researchers identify problems and possible improvements.

Thank you for your time and participation!

## Researcher Scoring Guide

This section is for the researchers and should not be shown while explaining individual questions to respondents.

The weighted mean may be calculated using:

\[
\bar{x}=\frac{\sum fx}{N}
\]

where \(\bar{x}\) is the weighted mean, \(f\) is the frequency of each response, \(x\) is the numerical response weight, and \(N\) is the number of valid responses.

| Mean range | Response | Interpretation |
|---:|---|---|
| 4.21–5.00 | Strongly Agree | Highly Acceptable |
| 3.41–4.20 | Agree | Acceptable |
| 2.61–3.40 | Neutral | Moderately Acceptable |
| 1.81–2.60 | Disagree | Less Acceptable |
| 1.00–1.80 | Strongly Disagree | Not Acceptable |

Suggested groupings:

- Items 1–5: Learnability and ease of use
- Items 6–10: Attendance and fingerprint use
- Items 11–15: Employee information, leave, and payroll
- Items 16–20: Speed, reliability, and security
- Items 21–25: Overall satisfaction

Before administration, qualified reviewers should examine the instrument for clarity, relevance, and alignment with the study. If a pilot test is conducted, calculate Cronbach's alpha using actual responses and report only the genuine result. Survey ratings measure user perceptions and should be interpreted together with interviews, observations, technical tests, and biometric evaluation results.
