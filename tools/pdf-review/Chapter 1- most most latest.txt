Background of the Study  
Employee attendance and work information management is 
a vital component of organizational operations, as it 
directly affects productivity, accountability, and decision-
making. Globally, organizations are increasingly adopting 
digital and analytics-driven human resource systems to 
replace manual and outdated attendance methods that often 
result in errors, inefficiencies, and limited access to 
actionable insights. Recent literature highlights that the 
integration of biometric-based system is revolutionizing 
human resource management by enhancing operational 
efficiency, minimizing labor-related inefficiencies, and 
eliminating the administrative burdens of traditional 
tracking methods (ljiga et al., 2025). Modern workforce 
analytics systems enable organizations to record attendance 
accurately, monitor work patterns, and generate data-driven 
reports that support effective human resource management.  
Despite these global advancements, studies reveal that 
many organizations still rely on disconnected or partly 
manual processes to manage employee information, attendance,

Many organizations manage employee information, 
attendance, leave, and payroll through disconnected or

Scope and Delimitation  
This study covers the design and development of 
WorkPulse, as a responsive, web-based workforce information 
management system utilizing a cloud-hosted MongoDB Atlas 
database. The scope includes an attendance kiosk equipped 
with a fingerprint scanner that allows employees to perform 
time-in and time-out through biometric identity 
verification, preventing unauthorized clock-ins. The system 
also encompasses administrator and employee authentication 
(via email OTP), role-based access control, employee 
information management, an employee self-service portal, 
attendance-record monitoring, leave-request processing, 
payroll workflows, and system settings. The system features 
descriptive dashboards and security audit event logging. 
Account roles are strictly limited to administrators and 
regular employees, with no separate portal for HR or middle 
management. 
However, the system has specific delimitations. The 
fingerprint-based attendance feature is limited to the

This study aims to contribute to improved workforce 
management and organizational efficiency. The system may 
benefit the following: 
Administrators and Management. Administrators can 
efficiently track verifiable employee attendance, manage 
leave approvals, process payroll workflows, and make 
informed decisions using centralized data dashboards hosted 
on a scalable cloud database. The system helps centralize 
workforce records, reduce repetitive administrative work, 
and provides a highly reliable method of confirming physical 
attendance through biometric integration.
