Chapter 3 
 DISCUSSION OF FINDINGS 
This chapter presents the findings and evaluation of 
WorkPulse: Employee Attendance and Work Analytics 
Information System for MVL. It covers the identified 
problems and requirements, implemented system modules, 
technical and user evaluation results, biometric 
verification performance, and operational and usability 
issues. Survey results are presented using frequencies, 
percentages, and weighted means, while interview and 
observation findings are summarized into recurring themes. 
Challenges and Issues of the Current System 
 Based on the identified existing processes, the 
current system of managing employee attendance and 
workforce information presents several challenges. The 
processes for employee information, attendance, leave, and 
payroll are partly manual or fragmented, which can affect 
the accuracy, efficiency, and accessibility of records. The 
following are the major challenges identified:

Inaccurate Attendance Recording. This issue was 
identified by 90% of the respondents. Inaccurate attendance 
records can occur during the current attendance process, 
making it difficult to maintain reliable employee 
attendance information. This can also affect the accuracy 
of monitoring and payroll-related records. 
Risk of Unauthorized or Proxy Attendance. Reported by 
93.33% of respondents, this issue reflects the concern that 
attendance may be recorded by someone other than the actual 
employee. This can compromise the accuracy and reliability 
of attendance records and may lead to incorrect employee 
attendance information. 
Inefficient Monitoring of Work Hours. A total of 70% 
of respondents experienced difficulties in monitoring 
employee work hours. Manual monitoring can make it 
challenging to accurately track working time, especially 
when dealing with multiple employees. This may result in 
inefficient monitoring and inaccurate work-hour records. 
Payroll Processing Difficulties. Reported by 90% of 
respondents, this issue reflects the challenges encountered 
in processing employee payroll. Manual handling of 
attendance and payroll information can increase the

Limited Employee Access to Records. This issue was

limited access to their attendance, leave, and other work-
related records. This can reduce transparency and may 
require employees to depend on administrators whenever they 
need to check or obtain their information. 
Features of the Proposed System 
 To address the identified challenges, WorkPulse: 
Employee Attendance and Work Analytics Information System 
for MVL is proposed as a responsive web-based workforce 
information management system. It centralizes employee, 
attendance, leave, and payroll-related information and 
provides separate services for administrators and 
employees. The proposed system includes the following 
features: 
Employee Information Management. The system provides a 
centralized employee directory where authorized 
administrators can manage employee information and maintain 
organized workforce records.

Computation of Results. The evaluation of the 
WorkPulse: Employee Attendance and Work Analytics 
Information System for MVL was based on three key system 
quality criteria, namely security, performance efficiency, 
and reliability. These criteria were used to assess the 
system’s ability to protect employee and organizational 
data, perform attendance and workforce-related processes 
efficiently, and operate consistently without errors. Each 
criterion was measured using multiple statements in the 
Likert Scale questionnaire. The participants’ responses

quality and effectiveness of the WorkPulse system. 
To determine the results, the mean was computed for 
each statement, while the weighted mean was used to obtain 
the overall rating for each evaluation category. The mean 
represents the average response of the respondents for each 
indicator, and the weighted mean represents the overall 
average for each criterion.
