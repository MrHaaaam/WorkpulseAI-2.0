Chapter 4 
 CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS  
This chapter presents the conclusions and 
recommendations derived from the findings of the study. The 
study focused on the development and evaluation of 
WorkPulse: Employee Attendance and Work Analytics 
Information System for MVL, a responsive web-based 
workforce information management system designed to address 
challenges in employee attendance, workforce information, 
leave, and payroll management. Based on the findings 
presented in the previous chapter, this section summarizes 
the major outcomes of the study and provides 
recommendations for further improvement and future 
development of the system. 
Conclusions 
 Based on the findings of the study, the following 
conclusions were drawn: 
1. The study successfully identified the major challenges 
encountered in the existing employee attendance and 
workforce information process. The findings revealed 
issues involving inaccurate attendance recording, the 
risk of unauthorized or proxy attendance, inefficient

monitoring of work hours, payroll processing 
difficulties, and limited employee access to records. 
These challenges demonstrate the need for a more 
centralized and reliable system for managing employee-

2. The WorkPulse system successfully implemented features 
that address the identified challenges in employee 
attendance and workforce information management. The 
system provides employee information management, 
fingerprint-based attendance, attendance monitoring, 
employee self-service, leave management, payroll 
management, payroll summary or payslip viewing, 
workforce analytics, role-based access control, secure 
authentication, audit logging, data validation and 
security, cloud-based data storage, and company and 
system settings. These features provide a centralized 
platform for managing workforce-related information 
and processes. 
3. The system demonstrated an acceptable level of 
usability based on the System Usability Scale 
evaluation. WorkPulse obtained a SUS score of 80.83, 
which was interpreted as Good and Acceptable. This

management, security controls, and workforce

accessible approach to managing employee-related 
processes.

Recommendations 
 Based on the conclusions and limitations of the study, 
the following recommendations are proposed: 
1. Future developers may further enhance the 
attendance module by improving fingerprint verification and 
support additional biometrics devices to provide greater 
flexibility and reliability in employee attendance 
recording.  
2. The attendance monitoring may feature may be 
further expanded by providing more advanced filtering 
reporting, and attendance summary options to help 
administrators analyze employee attendance and work-hour 
records more efficiency. 
3. Future improvements may include enhanced workforce 
analytics such as additional reports, trends, visualization 
features to provide administrators with more comprehensive 
information for workforce planning and decision-making. 
4. The employee self-service portal may be further 
enhanced by adding additional employee-accessible 
functions and notifications related to attendance, leave 
request, payroll information, and other authorized 
records.
